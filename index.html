How verification works

From application to a re-verification reminder — fully tracked end to end.

1

Trader applies

Register an instrument and submit a verification request online with the applicable fee.

2

Inspector assigned

The department assigns an inspector by district and schedules a site visit.

3

Verify & certify

The inspector records the result; a digital certificate and QR-checkable record are issued.

4

Track & renew

Expiry is tracked automatically and renewal reminders are sent before the due date.
